## Mapping and continuity decisions

- Case 1 and Case 10 classroom scenarios and their previously-approved executive dilemmas are **not overwritten**; the booklet is linked as a visible complementary learning lens. This avoids losing previous decisions about the first manager session.
- The manager session-one route (120 minutes, Cases 1 and 10, Medicator 5, Difficult Behavior 12, Values discussion) is preserved.
- Source pillars are linked to answers via **declared deterministic topic mapping**. The chart shows **recorded group discussions**, not individual voting share or verified organization-wide consensus.
- Existing dialogue records are **not rewritten** or discarded. The five-pillar proposal-review state has its own storage key to avoid interpreting legacy seven-category approvals as five-pillar approvals.
- Stored feedback is still local to the current browser; export is necessary for durable collection across different devices.

## Content quality guardrails

- A source-derived principle is distinguished from a fictional class case.
- Rules are discussed as working hypotheses for management practice; none is a legal/HSE authorization or a formally ratified policy.
- No personality labels for difficult colleagues.
- Real trade-offs (speed/accountability, relationships/fairness, autonomy/oversight, specialization/collaboration) and concrete sentences/actions.
- Visual content is meaningful and limited: five small pillar cards and one expandable group of eight rules; no new standalone dashboards or parallel navigation.
